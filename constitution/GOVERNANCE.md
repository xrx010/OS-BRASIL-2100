# Governance

## Purpose
This document defines the institutional governance model for the Genesis ecosystem and its Brazil 2100 agenda.

## Principles
- Transparency
- Traceability
- Participation
- Technical modularity
- Institutional accountability

## Roles
### Stewardship Council
Responsible for strategic direction, approval of constitutional revisions, and institutional alignment.

### Technical Core
Maintains the Genesis Platform, its repository architecture, and technical validity.

### Municipal and Pilot Networks
Test deployment, contribution, and local adaptation of solutions.

### Documentation and Knowledge Layer
Maintains architecture records, branding guidelines, and public-facing documentation.

## Decision Model
Decisions should be documented as versioned proposals, reviewed by stakeholders, and recorded in the ecosystem repository.

## Revision Cycle
The governance structure will review strategic direction periodically, with constitutional updates tracked through versioned documents in this folder.
