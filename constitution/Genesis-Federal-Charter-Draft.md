# Genesis Federal Charter Draft

## Scope
This draft describes a federal institutional framework for the Genesis ecosystem as a national knowledge and planning platform.

## Mission
Create a resilient institutional infrastructure that supports long-horizon planning, territorial intelligence, public policy coordination, and civic transparency.

## Strategic Objectives
- build national and regional planning capacity
- connect knowledge systems across sectors
- support municipal and institutional pilots
- maintain open and auditable digital public infrastructure

## Draft Articles
### Article 1 — Institutional legitimacy
The ecosystem shall operate as a public-interest digital infrastructure and shall preserve its technical and governance traceability.

### Article 2 — Shared stewardship
Responsibility for stewardship shall be distributed across institutional, academic, and municipal actors committed to shared values.

### Article 3 — Modularity
The platform shall continue to be modular, allowing incremental expansion without compromising the functional core.

### Article 4 — Public value
All major outputs must be useful for public planning, transparency, and civic access.

### Article 5 — Continuous evolution
The ecosystem shall evolve through documented revisions, community review, and institutional pilots.
