# Genesis Constitution v1

## Preamble
The Brasil 2100 ecosystem is a civic, scientific, and technological initiative for building a public intelligence layer for the Brazilian future.

## Purpose
This constitution establishes the institutional foundations of the Genesis ecosystem, ensuring that public knowledge, strategic planning, governance, and digital delivery remain open, auditable, and aligned with the common good.

## Core Principles
1. Knowledge as infrastructure.
2. Democratic stewardship of public data and strategic planning.
3. Modular, interoperable, and maintainable systems.
4. Open collaboration with institutional accountability.
5. Public value over proprietary lock-in.

## Institutional Scope
The ecosystem encompasses:
- the Genesis Platform core
- the Brasil 2100 project
- institutional and municipal pilots
- documents, architecture, and governance layers
- partnerships across public institutions, academia, and civic technology

## Governance
The governance model prioritizes transparent decision-making, metadata traceability, versioned policies, and accountable stewardship by institutions and stakeholders.

## Adoption
This charter is a foundational reference for future institutional, municipal, and thematic implementations of the Genesis ecosystem.
